# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, not the person.

## What we expect

- **Engage with the strongest version of what someone said.** If a comment is
  ambiguous, ask rather than assume the worst reading.
- **Criticize code, designs and decisions freely.** That is the point of a review
  tool. Criticize people, never.
- **Accept that disagreement ends.** Once a maintainer decides, say your piece
  once and move on. Re-litigating a settled decision in every thread is a form
  of obstruction.
- **Respect people's time.** Search before asking, include what you already
  tried, and say what you actually want.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind. Personal attacks, slurs, or demeaning comments about
someone's identity, background or ability. Sexual attention or imagery.
Publishing someone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussion.
Encouraging any of the above.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code, and any space
where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to **info@bikeshed.now**. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared further without your consent, except where we are legally obliged to. If your report is about a maintainer and you would rather it did not reach them, use [GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse), which does not pass through this project.

Tell us what happened, where, and when. Links help. You do not need to have a
solution in mind, and you do not need to be the target — reporting something you
witnessed is welcome.

## What happens next

We will acknowledge your report within a few days and tell you what we intend to
do. Depending on severity, that ranges from a private word, through a warning,
to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith face the same consequences as anyone else.
