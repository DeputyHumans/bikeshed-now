bikeshed.now

A guide

Put a Bikeshed document on your web page.

Show a document on a site of your own, such as a wiki, a product page or a status page, and keep it up to date by publishing here.

Change /d/ to /embed/ and put it in an iframe. Your page shows the latest version, and every reader is counted.

New to Bikeshed? How Bikeshed works explains the rest, and Bikeshed for developers covers agents and the command line.

01

What you can show

A document anyone may read: a public one, or one shared by link, with its key.

  • A public document, or one in a public folder, shows to anyone who opens your page.
  • A document shared as Anyone with the link needs its key on the address, as ?k= followed by the key from its link. Anyone who can read your page's source can then read the document, so only do this where that's fine.
  • A private document, one past its end date, or one in the Trash shows This document isn’t available here. So does an address that doesn't exist, so an embed never says whether a private document is there.
  • People you shared a private document with can't see it in an embed either, because another site's page never sends their sign-in. Open in Bikeshed, under the embed, takes them to it.

02

Add it to a page

Take the document's address, change /d/ to /embed/, and put that in an iframe.

If the document is at https://bikeshed.example/d/q3-plan, its embed is at https://bikeshed.example/embed/q3-plan:

<iframe src="https://bikeshed.example/embed/q3-plan"
        title="Q3 plan"
        width="100%" height="640"
        style="border: 0"></iframe>
  • Use this site's address in place of bikeshed.example.
  • It shows the latest version, and a new one reaches your page within five minutes. To keep one version, put it on the address: /embed/q3-plan/v3.
  • Give the iframe a title, which is what a screen reader announces.
  • Leave out sandbox. If your site needs it, use sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox". With less, the document still shows, but its links stop opening and its readers stop being counted.

03

Size and layout

Your page decides how big the embed is. The document scrolls inside it.

  • Set a height, because an iframe doesn't grow to fit what's in it. 640 pixels suits most documents, and a short note needs less.
  • For a box that keeps its shape as the page narrows, give the iframe width: 100%; height: auto; aspect-ratio: 4 / 3 in your stylesheet.
  • A link that leaves the document opens in a new tab, so your page stays where it was.
  • A deck shows as one long page. Slide by slide, presenting and the slide rail are in Bikeshed, one click away.

04

Who can see it

The embed follows the document's own sharing, so you change it in Share, never on your page.

  • Make the document private, give it an end date that passes, or move it to the Trash, and within five minutes the embed says it isn't available here.
  • Sharing a document by link again after making it private gives it a new key, and every embed that used the old one stops.
  • A document that was readable only because its folder is public stops showing when it moves out of that folder.
  • Any site can embed a public document, as any site can link to one. To keep a document off other sites, share it by link or keep it private.
  • Search engines are asked to leave the embed alone and find the document at its own address.

05

What counts as a read

Someone reading your page is reading the document, and is counted as any reader is.

  • An embed counts once it has been on screen for two seconds, in the part of your page the reader can see, in a tab they're looking at. An embed further down that nobody scrolls to isn't a read.
  • Reading time counts while the reader is active, as it does in Bikeshed.
  • Readers of an embed are counted without their names, even those with an account here, because another site's page never sends their sign-in.
  • The document's Activity says how many visits came through an embed, and marks each read that did.
  • Where this site measures use with analytics, the embed asks the reader first, once for each site that shows it. No title, address or contents are sent. Bikeshed's own count includes everyone, and analytics only those who said yes.

06

What an embed doesn't do

It shows the document. Everything else happens in Bikeshed.

No comments.
Comments, highlights and suggestions are in Bikeshed. Open in Bikeshed takes a reader to them.
No scripts.
A page's own scripts never run in an embed, even where Bikeshed runs them for everyone.
Not the file address.
The address a document's files are served from, under /content/, can be put in an iframe too, but it is fixed to one version, counts nothing, and its links don't open. Use /embed/.
Your page has the last word.
The page around an embed decides its size and what sits beside it or over it. Bikeshed can't change that, so readers trust your page as much as the document.
