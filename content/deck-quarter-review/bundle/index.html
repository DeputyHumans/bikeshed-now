The middle row is the one that matters. Everything else follows from somebody reading a document the day it appeared.

Shipped

  • Anchored comments across versions. A note keeps pointing at its passage after the document is revised.
  • Agent access. Feedback arrives from whatever is reading, and is marked as such.
  • Folders. Filing stopped being a person's job.

Stopped, deliberately

Two things came off the list, and both were the right call:

  • A search index. Nobody asked twice, and it would have doubled the storage story.
  • Real-time co-editing. The failure mode is silent data loss, and the demand was one team.

Both remain reversible. Neither is a gap.
